# NUMERATA — Due Diligence & Literature Search Report

**Project:** NUMERATA | **Phase:** 1 — Critical Analysis
**Date:** 2026-07-19
**Author:** QNFO Research

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This report presents the results of the Phase 1 due diligence and literature search for Project NUMERATA: a systematic framework for evaluating numeral systems along cognitive, educational, and design dimensions.

**Key findings:**
- **QNFO Cross-Reference:** 10 Silent Radix papers identified as prior art within the QNFO corpus. These provide the formal/logical foundation but do not address cognitive, educational, or design evaluation criteria.
- **External Literature:** 27 papers classified (6 core, 15 supporting, 6 background) spanning cognitive science, semiotics, notation history, and human factors.
- **Gap Analysis Verdict: NOVEL** — NUMERATA extends Silent Radix into cognitive/educational/design domains. No existing framework integrates all three evaluative axes.

### 2.2 Gap Analysis

- **What QNFO covers:** Formal properties of positional numeral systems, distinction-based formalisms, operational closure, graphical representation of SR concepts.
- **What QNFO does not cover:** Cognitive evaluation (embodied metaphors, cognitive load, learning trajectories), educational assessment (scaffolding, bootstrapping, curriculum integration), design heuristics (error prevention, visual discriminability, interface integration).
- **Verdict:** NUMERATA fills a genuine gap. The formal/logical analysis of Silent Radix provides a rigorous vocabulary; NUMERATA extends this into evaluation criteria that matter for human users and system designers.

## 5. Gap Analysis & Novelty Assessment

### 5.1 What Exists

- Formal analysis of positional systems (Silent Radix corpus)
- Cognitive neuroscience of numerical cognition (Dehaene, Nieder, Butterworth)
- Historical/structural typology of notations (Chrisomalis, Cajori)
- Zero semiotics (Rotman, Kaplan, Seife)

### 5.2 What Is Missing

- **Integrated evaluation framework** combining cognitive, educational, and design criteria
- **Empirical comparison** of numeral systems on human performance metrics
- **Design heuristics** derived from cognitive science for novel numeral system construction
- **Trade-off analysis** between competing desiderata (e.g., learnability vs. computational efficiency)

### 5.3 Novelty Verdict

**NOVEL** — NUMERATA synthesises three distinct literatures (cognitive science, notation history, human factors) that have not previously been integrated into a single evaluation framework for numeral systems. The QNFO Silent Radix programme provides the formal foundation; NUMERATA extends this into applied evaluation criteria.
